### What happened (written 2026-11-03)

**In one line:** a small ₹178 Cr railway-sleeper/pipe maker with a lukewarm IPO listed at a 2% discount, then slid on the lower circuit almost every day and is down about half from the ₹220 issue price.

**Timeline**
- **Sep 30 – Oct 5:** IPO open, price band ₹208–220, lot of 68 shares (₹14,960). Final subscription **1.79x** (QIB 1.33x, NII 1.89x, retail 1.75x). Weak for a year with mostly 10x+ IPOs.
- **GMP:** rose to ₹20 (about +9%) while bidding was open, then **collapsed to ₹2 (+0.9%) on the last day**. The grey market was already saying "no real demand".
- **Oct 8 (listing):** listed at **₹215 (−2.27%)**, closed about **−7%** (hit the 5% lower circuit at roughly ₹204). Reddit: "tried to sell on day one, no sellers".
- **Oct 9 – 17:** repeated lower-circuit days. One holder on Oct 17: "35% below IPO price", another: "stuck, nobody to buy".
- **Late Oct:** about **−50% from issue price** (our backtest data: −52.7%). One source shows ₹104 on Oct 30 (unconfirmed, check a live price).

**Why it fell: no confirmed trigger.** I found no bad news, regulatory action or fraud story. The pattern fits a thin-float small-cap with weak demand: low QIB interest, stock stuck in a circuit band with sellers but no buyers, retail allottees panic-selling. These are likely contributors, not proven causes.

**The business and numbers (looked fine on paper)**
- Makes PSC railway sleepers, precast concrete and MS pipes for pumped-storage projects; construction services.
- FY26: profit ₹25 Cr (FY25 ₹23.6 Cr, FY24 ₹3.5 Cr), ROE 34%, debt/equity 1.0, P/E about 17.
- Promoters sold ₹33 Cr (OFS) and went from 73% to 49%.
- Only one broker review, "Neutral". Of the ₹148.8 Cr raised, ₹75 Cr goes to working capital and ₹34 Cr to "general corporate purposes".

**What this tool said before listing**
- Listing verdict **Avoid (35)**, long-term **Avoid (45)**. Predicted gain +0.9% (range −5.7% to +4.9%); actual **−2.3%, inside the range**. Chance to beat same-quarter IPOs: 26%.
- Lesson: low subscription + the GMP collapsing on the final day + tiny size + promoter selling were all visible *before* listing. Good financials did not save it: demand and liquidity decided the outcome.
